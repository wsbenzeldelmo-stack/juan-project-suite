Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 20, 2026

JUAN PROJECT Online processes information needed to accept service requests, maintain client accounts, manage projects and payments, provide secure order tracking, and operate the portal.

Information We Process

This may include your name, email address, optional contact details, Order Request information, project and deliverable records, invoices, payment-submission details, account activity, uploaded payment proofs, and technical identifiers needed for security and portal functionality.

Device Storage

JUAN PROJECT Online uses browser storage for essential functions such as maintaining your cart, restoring sign-in sessions, remembering order-tracking information, avoiding repeated promotional popups, and saving privacy preferences. Anonymous in-house advertising events may also be recorded when allowed by your privacy preference.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to review requests, create and manage projects, verify payments, communicate project status, provide files, maintain account security, measure JUAN PROJECT’s own promotions, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot the platform.

Service Providers

The platform uses hosted infrastructure and database services to operate JUAN PROJECT Online and Workspace. Access to administrative credentials and privileged database functions is restricted to server-side systems.

Retention & Security

Records may be retained when needed for project history, financial records, dispute resolution, analytics, and platform security. JUAN PROJECT uses access controls, HTTPS, server-side authorization, input validation, and rate limiting, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can choose essential-only storage in the privacy banner. Logged-in clients may request corrections to inaccurate profile information. Order Requests and campaigns with operational or analytics history may be archived rather than permanently deleted to preserve audit records.

Questions

For privacy or account questions, contact JUAN PROJECT through the support options available in JUAN PROJECT Online.
